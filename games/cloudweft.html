How to play Cloudweft Passage

You are the courier of a small floating archipelago. Its paths have split into two overlapping worlds: SUN and MOON. Recover three dawn glyphs in each chapter and carry them to the blue portal on the final island. Three handcrafted chapters unlock in order, and completed chapters are available for practice.

Use WASD or the arrow keys to move, Space to jump, and E to fold between worlds. On touch screens, use the direction pad and Jump and Fold sky buttons. Amber bridges are solid in SUN, while blue bridges are solid in MOON. Pale bridge outlines are previews, not walkable surfaces. Change worlds from a broad island before crossing.

The second chapter introduces gaps and moving pink sentry lights. Jump across gaps and wait for a light to pass. The third chapter adds wider gaps and a gentle wind gate. Every island is a checkpoint. Falling returns you to the last island with collected items intact. There is no life limit or payment to retry.

Each chapter has four optional mint relics. Two are on the side island reached from the third main island. Scores start at 2,200, add 250 per relic, and subtract three per active second and 100 per rescue, with a minimum of 100. Gold requires all four relics, no rescues and under 160 seconds; up to three rescues earns silver otherwise. The daily route mirrors the course on some days and changes sentry timing. Progress and best scores stay on this device. Shared results are self-reported, with no online leaderboard.

Questions before your first flight

Is this a full 3D game?

Yes. The islands, bridges and courier are rendered as real 3D meshes with WebGL. The game runs in your browser without external game models.

What happens if I fall or close the page?

A fall returns you to the last island with collected items intact. Continue saved journey restores the latest checkpoint on this device. A new journey replaces that active save.

Can I play without ads or an account?

Yes. No account, payment or ad viewing is required to complete any chapter. Daily routes are replayable. Shared results are self-reported.
