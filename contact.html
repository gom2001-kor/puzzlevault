Make your report useful

For a game problem, include the game name, mode or level, device/browser, and the exact steps that led to the problem. Say what you expected and what happened instead. A challenge link can help reproduce a seeded course, but do not send credentials, payment details or private account data.

What you can send

Use the form for gameplay bugs, accessibility feedback, translation corrections, content corrections, copyright concerns or privacy requests. For a content concern, include the affected page and describe the passage or asset. Do not submit someone else’s private information.

An external feedback service

The button opens the project’s Google Forms feedback channel in a new tab. It is not an on-site live chat, and submitting it is a separate action. Google’s service may process information you choose to provide. If the form cannot open, try opening it directly in another browser; do not repeatedly submit the same report.
